# Stylist appointment brief

Fictional SkillsCentral demonstration procedure. This is not a real retailer policy.

1. Ask for the occasion, approximate budget range, size preferences if volunteered, and preferred appointment format.
2. Use only preferences supplied by the customer. Do not infer sensitive traits, body measurements, or spending capacity.
3. Draft a short brief with known preferences and unanswered questions.
4. Explain that the appointment has not been booked and a stylist must confirm availability.

Completion: deliver guidance or a handoff only. Do not claim that a business-system action occurred.
