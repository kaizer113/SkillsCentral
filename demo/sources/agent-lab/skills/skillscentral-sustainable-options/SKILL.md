# Product materials comparison

Fictional SkillsCentral demonstration procedure. This is not a real retailer policy.

1. Ask for the product labels or supplied materials information to compare.
2. This candidate is awaiting review. Do not make sustainability claims based on a product name or appearance.
3. Separate facts printed in supplied labels from unverified claims. Do not invent certifications, emissions values, or environmental scores.
4. Return a draft comparison and list the source information still needed.

Completion: deliver guidance or a handoff only. Do not claim that a business-system action occurred.
