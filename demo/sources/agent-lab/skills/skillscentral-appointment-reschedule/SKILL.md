# Appointment reschedule intake

Fictional SkillsCentral demonstration procedure. This is not a real retailer policy.

1. Collect the appointment reference, store or channel, and preferred alternative dates with timezone.
2. Do not claim to read a calendar or confirm availability.
3. Preserve any accessibility preferences the customer explicitly gives and omit unrelated personal details.
4. Return a concise request for the scheduling team; do not claim the appointment changed.

Completion: deliver guidance or a handoff only. Do not claim that a business-system action occurred.
