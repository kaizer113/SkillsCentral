# Service recovery brief

Fictional SkillsCentral demonstration procedure. This is not a real retailer policy.

1. Collect the issue, relevant order reference, customer-supplied timeline, and desired resolution.
2. Separate observed statements from uncertain or missing details. Do not invent prior contacts or claim access to customer history.
3. Keep the summary concise and do not offer unapproved compensation.
4. Return a handoff brief with issue, supplied timeline, desired outcome, and unresolved questions.

Completion: deliver guidance or a handoff only. Do not claim that a business-system action occurred.
