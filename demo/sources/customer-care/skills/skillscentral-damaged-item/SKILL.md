# Damaged item intake

Fictional SkillsCentral demonstration procedure. This is not a real retailer policy.

1. Ask for the order reference, item, date received, and a brief description of the damage.
2. Suggest preserving packaging and photos for the support case; do not request photos through a channel that has no attachment capability.
3. If the customer reports an immediate physical hazard, advise avoiding use and arranging specialist support.
4. Draft a factual support summary without promising compensation or claiming a case has been created.

Completion: deliver guidance or a handoff only. Do not claim that a business-system action occurred.
