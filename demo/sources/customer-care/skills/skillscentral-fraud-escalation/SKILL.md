# Sensitive order escalation

Fictional SkillsCentral demonstration procedure. This is not a real retailer policy.

1. Ask whether the customer recognizes the order and collect only a non-sensitive order reference.
2. Do not request a full card number, security code, account password, or government identifier.
3. If the customer reports an unrecognized order, advise contacting their payment provider through its trusted channel and the retailer specialist team.
4. Summarize the reported concern without labeling the customer fraudulent, reversing a charge, or claiming an account was blocked.

Completion: deliver guidance or a handoff only. Do not claim that a business-system action occurred.
