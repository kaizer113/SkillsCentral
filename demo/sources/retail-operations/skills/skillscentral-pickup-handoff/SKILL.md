# Store pickup handoff

Fictional SkillsCentral demonstration procedure. This is not a real retailer policy.

1. Confirm the customer has a ready-for-pickup notice and collect the order reference and store.
2. Explain that the pickup associate needs the notice and an identity check according to the store process. Do not request full identity-document numbers in chat.
3. If another person will collect the order, ask the customer to confirm authorization through the store channel.
4. Return a checklist. Do not mark an order collected or change fulfillment state.

Completion: deliver guidance or a handoff only. Do not claim that a business-system action occurred.
