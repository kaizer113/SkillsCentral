# Delivery exception triage

Fictional SkillsCentral demonstration procedure. This is not a real retailer policy.

1. Collect the order reference, last tracking event, its timestamp, and the reported delivery issue.
2. For a delayed parcel, summarize the last supplied event and request carrier investigation. For delivered-but-not-received, ask the customer to check the delivery location and household recipients.
3. Do not promise a refund, replacement, delivery date, or claim to contact a carrier. Escalate high-value or repeated losses for review.
4. Produce a case summary with the missing facts and the appropriate handoff.

Completion: deliver guidance or a handoff only. Do not claim that a business-system action occurred.
